Ken is a supporting character in the movie Barbie

He is,,

  • Funny
  • Oblivious
  • Silly
  • Patriotic
  • And in love!

Ken, being more of a side character, shows a very over the top acting of masculinity. In the movie about feminism, he is the antagonist. As well as the guy you probably don't want to hang around all the time. He is funny and has a good heart, even if he means well he still gets into shenanigans and trouble.

I enjoy Ken because although he is the antagonist, he is also the comedy of the movie. The scene featuring his song is a hilariously good summer fun scene, and the Barbie movie in general is a great summer movie. If you're looking for something to brighten up your day, id say Ken, and the Barbie Movie in general is a great place to start. It’s a good beginner Ryan Gosling movie!
